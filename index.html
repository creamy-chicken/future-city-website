About the competition

Official website

The Future City Competition is a national STEM competition where middle school students build, design, and present a model of a city at least 100 years in the future. The competition combines engineering, science, architecture, art, writing, speech, and teamwork into an interactive building project.

This year's theme is “Fire Resilient Future,” where students design a resilient future city that can prevent, protect, and recover from urban wildfires.

There are four main deliverables for the project.

(65 points)

There is the city model, where teams build a physical model of their city using recycled materials and include one moving part.

(54 points)

Then there is the city essay, where teams describe the unique attributes of their city and the solutions to the annual challenge.

(72 points)

Then there's the presentation/Q&A part of the competition, where teams, in 7 minutes, present their city and answer questions from a panel of judges.

(10 points)

Lastly, there's the project plan, where teams develop a project plan that helps them stay organized and focused.

Prizes

Teams that win their regional competition advance to the Finals (in Washington, DC). Future City provides round-trip transportation and hotel accommodations for the U.S. team’s three student presenters, educator, and mentor. Learn more about awards and prizes at Future City Finals here.

Research Lead: They fact check information, connect ideas to reality, and find already done solutions online.
